How “Yield” is calculated

Paid out is the cumulative payout total reported to the SDB index, in units of the token's direct quote asset. That quote can differ from the backing asset in the logo, so totals are not converted or comparable across quote assets; each token record names its unit. Est. 24h fee rate = indexed 24h volume × stacked transfer fee ÷ market cap, per dollar held, for the last 24h of indexed volume. It assumes the full stacked fee is distributed pro rata to holders; actual distribution may differ. If volume, fee or market cap is missing or zero, the estimate is shown as unavailable. Source: SDB index of the StonkFun public API. These are observations and estimates, not a promise, guarantee or investment advice.
